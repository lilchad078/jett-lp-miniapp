How to read this

Net low / high: average result after a low and a high cost estimate. A rule counts only if it is profitable after low cost on at least 3 tokens.

Win: share of trades that ended in profit. OOS: result on the later part of the data the rule was not built on.

Strong: enough tokens and a positive OOS result. Weak: profitable so far, not enough proof yet.

Near miss / Rejected in the feed: how the live v5 filter would have judged the token. Research tracks it either way.

Launch 60m: new pools, entry in the first hour. Bonus stage: young pools after a 60% drop from the high with a calm last hour. 1m check vs 5m: the same trades with stops on the 1-minute price, shown as the difference.

Main pool: the token's first or largest SOL DLMM pool. Top 10 ≤ 20%: top 10 holders own at most 20%. Seen late: the trigger had already passed when first seen.
